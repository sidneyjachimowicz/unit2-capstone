Company Security Policy
Overview
This policy establishes baseline security practices for all employees and systems to protect company and customer data.
Access Control
Access to internal systems is granted on a least-privilege basis, meaning employees receive only the permissions necessary for their role. Access requests must be approved by the requesting employee's manager and reviewed quarterly by IT security.
Password & Authentication
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on company accounts, including email, VPN, and any system containing customer data. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and rotated every 90 days.
Data Handling
Customer data must never be stored on personal devices or unapproved third-party services. All customer data at rest must be encrypted, and all data in transit must use TLS 1.2 or higher.
Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including lost devices, phishing attempts, or unauthorized access, must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery. The security team will triage and communicate next steps within 4 hours of a report.
Third-Party Vendors
Any new vendor with access to customer data must complete a security review before onboarding, and existing vendor access is reviewed annually.
Employee Training
All employees complete mandatory security awareness training upon hire and annually thereafter, covering phishing recognition, data handling, and incident reporting procedures.
