Customer Complaint Handling Process
Overview
This document describes how customer complaints are received, triaged, and resolved to maintain trust and improve our products and services.
Intake
Complaints may arrive via the support portal, email, phone, or social media. All complaints are logged in the ticketing system within 1 business hour of receipt, regardless of channel.
Triage & Severity
Each complaint is assigned a severity level:
Critical: Service outage or data issue affecting multiple customers. First response within 30 minutes.
High: Significant issue affecting a single customer's ability to use the product. First response within 4 hours.
Medium: Functional issue with a workaround available. First response within 1 business day.
Low: Cosmetic issue, feature request, or general feedback. First response within 3 business days.
Resolution
Support agents aim to resolve Medium and Low severity complaints directly. Critical and High severity complaints are escalated to the relevant engineering or product team, with the original support agent remaining the customer's point of contact throughout.
Customer Success Follow-Up
For any complaint resulting in a service credit, refund, or extended outage, Customer Success reaches out proactively within 48 hours of resolution to confirm satisfaction and identify any relationship risk.
Root Cause Review
All Critical severity complaints, and any complaint that recurs three or more times from different customers within a 30-day period, trigger a root cause review involving support, engineering, and product leadership.
Reporting
Complaint volume, severity distribution, and resolution times are reviewed monthly to identify systemic product or process issues.
