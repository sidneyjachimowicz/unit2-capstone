Expense Approval Policy
Purpose
This policy defines how employee expense requests are reviewed and approved, ensuring appropriate financial oversight while keeping the process efficient for routine, low-cost purchases.
Submission
All expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of purchase, with an itemized receipt attached and a department code specified.
Approval Thresholds
Expenses of $500.00 or less: Automatically approved upon submission, provided a valid receipt and department code are attached. No manager sign-off is required.
Expenses over $500.00: Require manager sign-off before reimbursement is processed. The direct manager of the submitting employee must review and approve the request in the finance portal within 5 business days.
Expenses over $5,000.00: Require both manager sign-off and finance director approval, regardless of department.
Department Budget Codes
Every expense must be tagged with one of the following department codes at submission: Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Operations, or HR. Requests submitted without a valid department code will be returned to the submitter for correction and are not considered "submitted" until resubmitted correctly — this delay does not count against the standard reimbursement timeline.
Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days of final approval. Expenses requiring manager or director sign-off may take longer if approvers do not act within the 5-business-day review window.
Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (outside of approved client entertainment), personal subscriptions, and fines or penalties of any kind are not eligible for reimbursement under this policy.
